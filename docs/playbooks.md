# Start with a merchant decision

Install the skills you need and pass the previous step's evidence into the next. These recipes are optional sequences, not mandatory dependencies. Do not run every skill against every store.

## Prepare a product launch

1. [Product opportunity research](../skills/product-opportunity-research/SKILL.md): choose test/defer/reject using product, market and competitor evidence.
2. [Landed cost](../skills/landed-cost-model/SKILL.md): model actual purchasing, freight and import assumptions before setting a price.
3. [Shopify product launch](../skills/shopify-product-launch/SKILL.md): turn approved facts into a reviewable listing and supported draft.
4. [Launch marketing](../skills/dtc-launch-marketing/SKILL.md): plan assets, channel sequence and readiness checks against inventory and budget.

Handoff: one product fact sheet, cost worksheet, listing draft and launch readiness sheet. Unknown costs remain unknown; a positive opportunity review does not authorize procurement or publication.

## Repair paid-traffic performance

1. [GA4 ecommerce measurement](../skills/ga4-ecommerce-measurement/SKILL.md): verify the purchase event contract and duplicate handling.
2. [Ad landing-page message match](../skills/ad-landing-page-message-match/SKILL.md): compare the ad's offer and the actual destination.
3. [Creative fatigue diagnosis](../skills/creative-fatigue-diagnosis/SKILL.md): separate creative wear from audience, auction and tracking changes.
4. [Ad budget pacing](../skills/ad-budget-pacing/SKILL.md): make a dated spend forecast using the remaining authorized budget.

Handoff: event evidence, page/ad comparisons, competing explanations and a proposed spend schedule. Platform-attributed revenue is not automatically incremental revenue.

## Fix shopping-feed visibility

1. [Feed disapproval triage](../skills/shopping-feed-disapproval-triage/SKILL.md): classify the actual issue codes and affected products.
2. [Product feed optimization](../skills/product-feed-optimization/SKILL.md): prepare factual feed corrections matched to landing-page price, availability and variants.
3. [Google Shopping campaign audit](../skills/google-shopping-campaign-audit/SKILL.md): assess campaign structure and spend only after feed eligibility is understood.

Handoff: issue ledger, before/after field corrections and verification steps. Never invent GTINs, stock or policy evidence to force eligibility.

## Improve repeat purchasing

1. [Cohort retention](../skills/cohort-retention-analysis/SKILL.md): compare customers with equal observation windows.
2. [RFM segmentation](../skills/customer-segmentation-rfm/SKILL.md): produce auditable customer segments with explicit order/refund definitions.
3. [Post-purchase email](../skills/post-purchase-email-flow/SKILL.md) or [winback email](../skills/winback-email-flow/SKILL.md): choose the lifecycle flow supported by the diagnosis.
4. [Email deliverability](../skills/email-deliverability-audit/SKILL.md): check sending evidence before increasing volume.

Handoff: cohort table, eligible segment, flow draft and send QA. Do not infer consent from a purchase or send a campaign merely because the draft is ready.

## Free cash trapped in inventory

1. [Inventory aging](../skills/inventory-aging-clearance/SKILL.md): reconcile units and cost by SKU and receipt age.
2. [Discount profitability](../skills/discount-profitability/SKILL.md): compare clearance offers using contribution, returns and cannibalization assumptions.
3. [Replenishment planning](../skills/inventory-replenishment-planning/SKILL.md): reduce repeat over-ordering with lead-time demand and stock-position evidence.
4. [Inventory cash flow](../skills/cash-flow-inventory-planning/SKILL.md): sequence deposits, settlements and operating cash needs by date.

Handoff: aged-stock register, offer economics, proposed purchase quantities and dated cash forecast. A margin-positive order can still create a cash shortage.

## Learn from returns and service requests

1. [Support triage](../skills/customer-support-triage/SKILL.md): identify the request, authorized facts and next owner.
2. [Returns reason analysis](../skills/returns-reason-analysis/SKILL.md): connect comparable return cohorts and reason codes to products and variants.
3. [Product-page CRO](../skills/shopify-product-page-cro/SKILL.md): clarify the specifications or expectations that the evidence shows are confusing.
4. [Support knowledge base](../skills/support-knowledge-base/SKILL.md): write accurate, versioned help articles from approved policy.

Handoff: triage queue, evidence-backed root causes, replacement copy and help drafts. Customer complaints are evidence to investigate, not proof of a defect or permission to refund.

## Prepare a catalog update

1. [SKU and option identity](../skills/sku-and-option-identity-audit/SKILL.md): resolve identifiers and option collisions before editing variants.
2. [Product attribute schema](../skills/product-attribute-schema-audit/SKILL.md): check owner, type, units and controlled values against the actual destination.
3. [Catalog import preflight](../skills/catalog-import-change-preflight/SKILL.md): create a minimal change file, before/after manifest and reconciliation plan.

Handoff: stable record IDs, reviewed field mappings and an import proposal. Omitted fields, explicit values and clearing a field have different meanings; verify the actual importer before execution.

## Reconcile cash before closing the month

1. [Payment and payout reconciliation](../skills/payment-payout-reconciliation/SKILL.md): trace settlement transaction IDs to payouts, bank deposits and order references.
2. [Bank and ledger reconciliation](../skills/merchant-bank-ledger-reconciliation/SKILL.md): separate timing items from supported proposed bookkeeping corrections.
3. [Month-end close packet](../skills/merchant-month-end-close-pack/SKILL.md): assemble reconciliations, inventory evidence, pending adjustments and reviewer decisions.

Handoff: control totals, evidence-linked exceptions and a close packet. Matching a payout amount does not prove every order link, and preparing the packet does not close or lock the books.

## Turn shopper research into product information

1. [Consumer interview planning](../skills/consumer-interview-planning/SKILL.md): prepare neutral questions about actual purchase episodes.
2. [Qualitative synthesis](../skills/qualitative-research-synthesis/SKILL.md): connect themes to participant evidence and contrary cases.
3. [Brand proof library](../skills/brand-proof-library/SKILL.md): preserve claim scope, source, permission and approved variants before using a finding in copy.

Handoff: a discussion guide, source-linked findings and verified proof units. An unfielded guide has no findings; a small interview sample does not establish market prevalence.

## Prepare creator content for paid use

1. [Creator rights review](../skills/creator-rights-review/SKILL.md): reconcile asset, placement, territory, dates and permitted edits.
2. [Creator content acceptance](../skills/creator-content-acceptance/SKILL.md): compare the actual version with product facts and the agreed brief.
3. [Creator paid amplification](../skills/creator-paid-amplification/SKILL.md): prepare an eligible asset plan with a matching destination, capped test and rights expiry.

Handoff: a rights schedule, accepted asset version and activation proposal. An organic repost grant does not establish paid or creator-identity advertising rights.
